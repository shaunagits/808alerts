WHAT THE LABELS MEAN

WARNINGHappening or about to. Act now. WATCHPossible soon. Get ready. ADVISORYLess serious, still take care. STATEMENTUpdates and context. NOTICEGovernment action: closures, shelters. ENDEDCancelled or expired.

808ALERTS

808 Alerts gathers official alerts in one place. It is not an official alert system. Every card links to the agency that issued it. In an emergency, call 911.
